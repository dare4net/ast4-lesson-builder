# The Disaster Committee: Master Lesson Plan Document

## Lesson 1 — Chapter 1: The Biscuit Tin Dilemma

**Curriculum Skills Taught:**
- Identifying and applying Aristotle's Triad of Persuasion (**Ethos, Pathos, Logos**).
- Distinguishing between credibility (ethos), emotional appeal (pathos), and facts (logos).

**Narrative Beat:** Committee stuck in endless tea break while storm approaches. Junior Persuasion Officer uses Ethos/Pathos/Logos to persuade Captain Bluster to issue a Yellow Alert.

**Handoff Type:** Planted Suspicion  
**Handoff Text:** *"As Captain Bluster agrees to put the committee on yellow alert, a sudden shudder rattles the windowpanes. A metallic scraping sound echoes from deep underground... accompanied by an ancient leather map marked 'BUNKER 7: SEAWALL OVERRIDE'."*

**Slide Breakdown:**
- Slide 1: `heading`, `paragraph`, `image` (Town Hall assembly), `accordion` (Committee member profiles)
- Slide 2: `heading`, `callout` (Ethos/Pathos/Logos definition), `matchingPairs` (Pairing pillars to definitions)
- Slide 3: `heading`, `categorise` (Sorting quotes into Ethos/Pathos/Logos)
- Slide 4: `heading`, `fillInTheBlank` (Inserting modal verbs and authoritative titles into yellow alert memo)
- Slide 5: `heading`, `shortAnswer` (Writing a 3-sentence address to activate Yellow Alert)
- Slide 6: `heading`, `callout` (Handoff text & ground shudder)

## Lesson 2 — Chapter 2: The Art of the Opening Hook

**Curriculum Skills Taught:**
- Writing high-impact opening hooks for speeches and digital bulletins.
- Employing Rhetorical Questions, Shocking Facts, Emotive Calls, and Dramatic Contrast.

**Narrative Beat:** Public ignoring dull council notices. You design High Street digital billboard bulletins using 4 hook engines.

**Handoff Type:** Discovery Object  
**Handoff Text:** *"As the new digital bulletin lights up High Street, forcing walking locals to pause, Gerald Nibbs drops his leather briefcase. Out rolls a handwritten note from Chief Engineer Barnaby Vane: 'BUNKER 7 LOCKED. I WILL NOT RISK MY BLUEPRINTS FOR A WAVE.' Gerald kicks it under a radiator."*

**Slide Breakdown:**
- Slide 1: `heading`, `hotspot` (Inspecting 4 failed paper notices around town square)
- Slide 2: `heading`, `flashcards` (Studying the 4 Hook Engines)
- Slide 3: `heading`, `trueFalse` (Evaluating candidate headlines for hook strength)
- Slide 4: `heading`, `dragDrop` (Sequencing billboard hook components: Hook → Context → Call to Action)
- Slide 5: `heading`, `shortAnswer` (Drafting a 2-sentence digital billboard hook)
- Slide 6: `heading`, `image` (Discovery handoff: secret Vane note under radiator)

## Lesson 3 — Chapter 3: Deconstructing Counter-Arguments & Conceding Points

**Curriculum Skills Taught:**
- Structuring counter-arguments (Acknowledge → Rebut → Redirect).
- Using concessive transitions (`While...`, `Although...`, `Even though...`).

**Narrative Beat:** Chief Engineer Barnaby Vane barricades Bunker 7, refusing to open the relief valve to protect his historic blueprint archives. You deliver a concessive counter-argument to persuade Vane to step aside.

**Handoff Type:** Character Twist  
**Handoff Text:** *"Vane steps aside from the tunnel door, but holds up a biometric scanner: 'I may be stepping aside, Officer, but you won't get past Guard Martha Vance at the vault door without signed municipal authorization or a speech that moves mountains.'"*

**Slide Breakdown:**
- Slide 1: `heading`, `image` (Barnaby Vane at tunnel entrance), `paragraph` (Vane's refusal)
- Slide 2: `heading`, `accordion` (Concessive transition rules & 3-step framework)
- Slide 3: `heading`, `fillInTheBlank` (Completing concessive sentences: "While your archives...", "Although the wall...")
- Slide 4: `heading`, `categorise` (Sorting statements into Acknowledge, Rebut, or Redirect)
- Slide 5: `heading`, `shortAnswer` (Drafting a 3-sentence concessive rebuttal to Vane)
- Slide 6: `heading`, `callout` (Handoff text: Guard Martha Vance warning)

## Lesson 4 — Chapter 4: The Infiltration of Bunker 7 (Fact vs. Opinion & Rhetorical Devices)

**Narrative Beat:** Guard Martha Vance blocks the vault entrance. You audit Spindle's data vs. Gerald's panic blogs (Fact vs. Opinion) and deliver a triad-powered address to persuade Vance to swipe her keycard.

**Slide Breakdown:**
- Slide 1: `heading`, `image` (Guard Vance at vault door), `hotspot` (Inspecting security panel)
- Slide 2: `heading`, `categorise` (Sorting Spindle & Gerald statements into Fact, Opinion, or Bias)

- Slide 5: `heading`, `shortAnswer` (Drafting a 3-sentence speech with Triads & Anaphora to Vance)
- Slide 6: `heading`, `callout` (Vault opening & jammed valve reveal)

## Lesson 5 — Chapter 5: Crafting the Persuasive Speech (Ethos & Pacing)

**Curriculum Skills Taught:**
- Structuring a 5-part classical speech (Exordium, Narration, Argument, Refutation, Peroration).
- Using modal verbs of urgency (*must*, *shall*) and discourse markers.

**Narrative Beat:** Mayor Flump video-calls from Tenerife dismissing the storm. You write a formal speech to persuade Captain Bluster to override Mayor Flump and trigger a town evacuation to Bunker 7.

**Handoff Type:** Reversal  
**Handoff Text:** *"Captain Bluster slams her fist onto the desk and authorizes the town evacuation! But just as the sirens blare, Professor Spindle checks her monitors: 'The North Pier mortar has dissolved! Seawater is flooding High Street and cutting off the main bridge to Bunker 7!'"*

**Slide Breakdown:**
- Slide 1: `heading`, `image` (Mayor Flump on video call from Tenerife), `paragraph` (Mayor's dismissal)
- Slide 2: `heading`, `timeline` (Sequencing the 5 speech sections)
- Slide 3: `heading`, `quiz` (Selecting high-urgency modal verbs: *must*, *shall*)
- Slide 4: `heading`, `multiSelectQuiz` (Evaluating draft speech sentences for discourse markers)
- Slide 5: `heading`, `shortAnswer` (Writing a 4-sentence evacuation speech to Bluster)
- Slide 6: `heading`, `callout` (North Pier breach reversal handoff)

## Lesson 6 — Chapter 6: The Emergency Broadcast (Adapting Tone & Medium)

**Curriculum Skills Taught:**
- Adapting persuasive tone across media (Radio broadcast vs Print notice).
- Writing concise imperative instructions under strict word limits.

**Narrative Beat:** High Street is flooding. You write a live loudspeaker radio broadcast script under strict word limits to direct citizens along the higher ridge path to Bunker 7 without causing a stampede.

**Handoff Type:** Hard Cliffhanger  
**Handoff Text:** *"Your broadcast succeeds! 50 townspeople rush down the ridge path into Bunker 7 just as floodwaters lap against the vault entrance. But inside the chamber, the citizens freeze in fear as the rusted iron chains creak under pressure!"*

**Slide Breakdown:**
- Slide 1: `heading`, `image` (High Street flooding, loudspeaker tower), `paragraph` (Emergency crisis)
- Slide 2: `heading`, `matchingPairs` (Matching media formats to stylistic rules)
- Slide 3: `heading`, `wordScramble` (Unscramble imperative codes: `EVACUATE`, `RIDGE`, `PROCEED`)
- Slide 4: `heading`, `fillInTheBlank` (Completing a 40-word concise radio script template)
- Slide 5: `heading`, `shortAnswer` (Writing a 3-sentence live loudspeaker broadcast)
- Slide 6: `heading`, `callout` (Citizens arrive at vault, chains creak cliffhanger)

## Lesson 7 — Chapter 7: The Grand Debate & Town Charter (Capstone Synthesis)

**Curriculum Skills Taught:**
- Synthesizing all persuasive devices (Ethos/Pathos/Logos, Hooks, AFOREST, Concessions, Triads, Audience Adaptation).
- Delivering a capstone formal persuasive declaration.

**Narrative Beat:** Inside the vault, panic breaks out as citizens hesitate to grab the freezing iron chains. You deliver the Wittling Rescue Declaration, uniting Bluster, Vane, Vance, Nibbs, and the town to heave the chains together and open the valve!

**Handoff Type:** Student Challenge  
**Handoff Text:** *"Safe on dry ground, Captain Bluster hands you the Wittling Silver Clipboard: 'You saved our town with your words, Chief Communications Officer. Now... how are you going to persuade Mayor Flump to pay for the new seawall?'"*

**Slide Breakdown:**
- Slide 1: `heading`, `paragraph` (Vault chamber, citizens hesitating before iron chains)
- Slide 2: `heading`, `spinTheWheel` (Gamified master review of all persuasive tools)
- Slide 3: `heading`, `categorise` (Sorting 6 excerpt sentences into Hook, Ethos, Logos, Concession, Triad, Peroration)
- Slide 4: `heading`, `memoryGrid` (Matching persuasive technique terms to definition & effect)
- Slide 5: `heading`, `shortAnswer` (Writing the 5-sentence Wittling Rescue Declaration capstone)
- Slide 6: `heading`, `image` (50 citizens, Bluster, Vane, Vance pulling chains together as valve opens and water drains)
- Slide 7: `heading`, `callout` (Graduation praise & Silver Clipboard award)
