# Lesson 4 Plan — The Infiltration of Bunker 7

**Series:** KS3 English — Persuasive & Argumentative Writing  
**Topic Slug:** `ks3-disaster-committee`  
**Chapter Title:** Chapter 4: The Infiltration of Bunker 7  

**Curriculum Skills Taught:**
- Employing Rhetorical Triads (Rule of Three) for rhythmic persuasive impact.
- Utilizing Anaphora (repeating key opening phrases across consecutive clauses/sentences).
- Integrating emotional appeal (Pathos) with institutional authority (Ethos) under extreme urgency.
- Synthesizing persuasive devices into a high-stakes speech.

**Narrative Beat:** Chief Engineer Barnaby Vane has locked himself inside the subterranean vault of Bunker 7, home to the emergency sea wall drainage override valve. Chief Security Guard Martha Vance stands outside the heavy iron vault door, refusing entry without Mayor Flump's signed authority. You must use a powerful persuasive speech to convince Guard Vance to override protocol and unlock Bunker 7 before the rising tide breaches the inner sea wall.

**Handoff Type:** Discovery Climax  
**Handoff Text:** *"As the heavy iron door of Bunker 7 grinds open, you step into the damp shadows. Engineer Vane is not destroying the system—he is standing beside a massive brass wheel valve labeled 'GRANDFATHER OVERRIDE 1922'. Tears stream down his face as he whispers: 'I wasn't hiding a conspiracy... I was trying to open it alone, but the wheel is jammed!'"*

**Design Principle:** **Dynamic Component Diversity.** This lesson intentionally avoids repeating the component structures of Lessons 1, 2, and 3. It introduces `hotspot` vault inspection, `flashcardQuiz`, `fillInTheBlank`, `categorise`, `shortAnswer`, and `poll` to maintain a fresh visual and interactive flow.

2. **`paragraph`**
   - **Props/Content outline:** Guard Vance hesitates, her hand hovering over the keycard scanner. "Give me one valid reason that justifies breaking protocol!" You must combine Authority (Ethos), Emotion (Pathos), and Logic (Logos) to overcome her hesitation.
   - **Pedagogical purpose:** Task scenario context.
   - **Narrative function:** Framing the tri-fold appeal.
   - **Placement rationale:** Intro paragraph before sorting.

3. **`categorise`**
   - **Props/Content outline:** Sort 3 speech lines into their persuasive appeal categories:
     - Category 1 (`cat-ethos`): `Ethos (Authority & Duty)`
     - Category 2 (`cat-pathos`): `Pathos (Emotion & Protection)`
     - Category 3 (`cat-logos`): `Logos (Logic & Sensor Facts)`
     - Item 1: `"As Disaster Committee Officers, we take full legal responsibility."` ➔ Category: `Ethos (Authority & Duty)`
     - Item 2: `"Think of the families asleep in East Pier who will lose their homes!"` ➔ Category: `Pathos (Emotion & Protection)`
     - Item 3: `"Pressure Gauge 3 reads 95%—the wall will breach in 12 minutes."` ➔ Category: `Logos (Logic & Sensor Facts)`
   - **Pedagogical purpose:** Interactive sorting of persuasive appeal types.
   - **Narrative function:** Preparing multi-angled arguments for Vance.
   - **Placement rationale:** Central interactive sorting component of Slide 4.
   - **`markingMode` & rationale:** `auto-mark`.

4. **`trueFalse`**
