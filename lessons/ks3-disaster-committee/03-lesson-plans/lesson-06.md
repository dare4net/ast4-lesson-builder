# Lesson 6 Plan — The Emergency Broadcast

**Series:** KS3 English — Persuasive & Argumentative Writing  
**Topic Slug:** `ks3-disaster-committee`  
**Lesson Slug:** `lesson-6`  
**Lesson Title:** The Emergency Broadcast (Adapting Tone & Medium)  

---

## 🎯 Curriculum Skills Taught
- Adapt persuasive and informative writing across different media formats (Radio Broadcast, Print Notice, SMS Alert, Digital Billboard).
- Write concise, imperative emergency instructions under strict word limits.
- Avoid panic-inducing loaded words while maintaining urgency and authority.

---

## 📖 Narrative Beat
Seawater breaches the North Pier wall, flooding High Street and cutting off the main bridge to Bunker 7. You must write a live radio/loudspeaker broadcast script under strict word limits to direct citizens along the higher ridge path to Bunker 7 without causing stampedes.

---

## 🔗 Handoff
**Handoff Type:** Hard Cliffhanger  
**Handoff Text:** *"Your broadcast succeeds! 50 townspeople rush down the ridge path into Bunker 7 just as floodwaters lap against the vault entrance. But inside the chamber, the citizens freeze in fear as the rusted iron chains creak under pressure!"*
