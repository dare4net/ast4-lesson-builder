# Lesson 7 Plan — The Grand Debate & Town Charter (Capstone)

**Series:** KS3 English — Persuasive & Argumentative Writing  
**Topic Slug:** `ks3-disaster-committee`  
**Lesson Slug:** `lesson-7`  
**Lesson Title:** The Grand Debate & Town Charter (Capstone Synthesis)  

---

## 🎯 Curriculum Skills Taught
- Synthesize all persuasive and argumentative techniques across the curriculum (Ethos/Pathos/Logos, Hooks, AFOREST, Concessions, Triads, Audience Adaptation, Speechcraft).
- Deconstruct exemplary persuasive text structures.
- Construct a comprehensive 5-sentence formal persuasive declaration (The Wittling Rescue Declaration).

---

## 📖 Narrative Beat
Trapped inside Bunker 7 while floodwaters rise outside, panic threatens to break out as citizens hesitate to grab the freezing iron chains connected to the rusted Grandfather Relief Valve. You step onto the valve platform to deliver the capstone Wittling Rescue Declaration. Inspired by your speech, Captain Bluster, Engineer Vane, Guard Vance, Gerald Nibbs, and 50 citizens heave the chains together, open the valve, drain the storm surge, and save Wittling-on-Sea!

---

## 🔗 Handoff
**Handoff Type:** Student Challenge / Course Graduation  
**Handoff Text:** *"Safe on dry ground, Captain Bluster hands you the official Wittling Silver Clipboard: 'You've mastered the art of persuasion, Chief Communications Officer. Now... how are you going to persuade Mayor Flump to pay for the new seawall?'"*
