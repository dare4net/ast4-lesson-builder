# Lesson 5 Plan — The Grand Evacuation Address

**Series:** KS3 English — Persuasive & Argumentative Writing  
**Topic Slug:** `ks3-disaster-committee`  
**Chapter Title:** Chapter 5: The Grand Evacuation Address (Series Finale)  

**Curriculum Skills Taught:**
- Structuring a full 4-part persuasive speech (Hook → Ethos/Logos Evidence → Concessive Refutation → Peroration).
- Employing a high-impact Peroration (final memorable summary and emotive call to action).
- Synthesizing all persuasive devices (Rhetorical Triads, Anaphora, Direct Address, Ethos/Pathos/Logos).
- Delivering a climactic persuasive address under extreme time pressure.

**Narrative Beat:** Inside Bunker 7, Engineer Barnaby Vane is straining against the massive rusted brass wheel valve labeled "GRANDFATHER OVERRIDE 1922". The valve is jammed—it requires 50 townspeople pulling the iron chains together! The storm surge is 10 minutes away. You must broadcast the Grand Evacuation Address over the High Street digital speakers, rallying the entire town of Wittling-on-Sea to rush to Bunker 7 and save the town!

**Handoff Type:** Series Resolution  
**Handoff Text:** *"As your voice echoes across Wittling High Street, 50 townspeople drop their sandbags and sprint into the corridor! Joined hand-in-hand, Captain Bluster, Professor Spindle, Gerald Nibbs, and the citizens heave the iron chains together. With a thunderous groan, the grand relief valve spins open—and the sea water drains safely into the lower reservoir. Wittling-on-Sea is saved!"*

**Design Principle:** **Dynamic Component Diversity.** This lesson concludes the series with a distinct component sequence (`timeline`, `matchingPairs`, `multiSelectQuiz`, `dragDrop`, `shortAnswer`, `poll`). Zero HTML tags and zero filler.

   - **Placement rationale:** Top anchor.

2. **`quote`**
   - **Props/Content outline:** `"Speech Opening: 'Citizens of Wittling! Category 3 winds batter our coast, but our courage will not break! While fear is natural, unity is our shield!'"`

3. **`multiSelectQuiz`**
   - **Props/Content outline:** Question: "Select the TWO strongest Peroration endings to conclude your speech and rally citizens to Bunker 7:"
     - Option 1: "We need your hands, we need your strength, and we need your unity at Bunker 7 right now!" (CORRECT)
     - Option 2: "Maybe come down if you aren't too busy with tea." (INCORRECT - Weak)
     - Option 3: "Together we stand, together we pull, together we save Wittling-on-Sea!" (CORRECT)
     - Option 4: "You should probably read Notice 4B again." (INCORRECT - Boring)
     - Explanation: "Options 1 and 3 use Rule of Three triads and Anaphora ('We need...', 'Together we...') to create an irresistible, rhythmic call to action!"
