# The Disaster Committee: Curated Video Clips Guide

**Series:** KS3 English — Persuasive & Argumentative Writing (`ks3-disaster-committee`)  
**Total Series Video Clips:** 3 Strategic Story Anchors  

---

## 📹 Purpose of Video Clips in Narrative Lessons

Video clips are **not** used for routine teaching or repetitive slide decorations. Across a multi-lesson narrative series, video clips serve strictly as **strategic visual anchors**:

1. **World & Story Opener (Lesson 1):** Establishes the environment, tone, and high-stakes premise instantly so learners care about the story world.
2. **Mid-Series Pivot Point (Lesson 4):** Visually anchors a major narrative turning point or physical crisis when the stakes escalate.
3. **Climactic Resolution (Lesson 7):** Provides a visual payoff during the final capstone resolution.

All other lessons rely on interactive discovery components (`hotspot`, `timeline`, `categorise`, `matchingPairs`, `quiz`, etc.) rather than video clips.

## 🎬 Curated Video Clips (3 Total Across Series)

### Clip 1: World & Premise Opener — *The Storm Surge at Bunker 7*
- **Placement:** Lesson 1 (Slide 1 — Scene 1: The Approaching Surge)
- **Story Purpose:** Sets the dramatic, dark cinematic tone of Wittling-on-Sea and introduces the looming threat of the Category 4 storm surge outside Bunker 7.
- **Duration:** 15–20 seconds (Looping background / motion hook)

#### 🎥 AI Visual Prompt (Text-to-Video Engine)
> `Cinematic wide angle, dark atmospheric storm surge battering an ancient stone sea wall at twilight. Massive dark ocean waves crashing against weathered iron vault doors labeled 'Bunker 7'. Water splashing over cobblestone walkways, coastal rain, moody blue and amber emergency lantern lighting, hyper-realistic, photorealistic 8k, volumetric fog, dramatic camera slow tilt upward toward iron reinforced sea gates, 35mm film grain.`

#### 🎙️ Audio / Voiceover Prompt (TTS Script)
> `[Sound: Thunder rumble, heavy ocean waves crashing, wind howling] Narrator (grave, authoritative tone): "High tide is approaching Wittling-on-Sea. Outside Bunker 7, ocean surge levels have breached safety margins. Fifty citizens wait inside..."`

---

### Clip 2: Mid-Series Climax — *The Subterranean Vault & Grandfather Valve*
- **Placement:** Lesson 4 (Slide 1 — Scene 1: The Vault Breach)
- **Story Purpose:** Visually anchors the physical crisis beneath Bunker 7 when water begins leaking into Room 4B, forcing the committee to confront physical reality versus political denial.
- **Duration:** 15–20 seconds

#### 🎥 AI Visual Prompt (Text-to-Video Engine)
> `Interior shot of a dark subterranean iron bunker vault. Rusted iron gears, massive copper steam pipes leaking white vapor, heavy iron chains hanging from a ceiling pulley system. In the center, a 10-foot brass relief wheel labeled 'Grandfather Valve'. Water leaking onto metallic floor, flickering warm tungsten industrial lights, cinematic slow pan left across the icy iron chains, highly detailed metallic textures, photorealistic lighting, shallow depth of field.`

#### 🎙️ Audio / Voiceover Prompt (TTS Script)
> `[Sound: Metallic creaking, water dripping, steam hiss] Narrator (intense, urgent): "Deep beneath the sea wall lies the Grandfather Relief Valve. Rusted shut for thirty years, it requires one hundred hands pulling together to spin..."`

---

### Clip 3: Climactic Resolution — *Opening the Relief Valve*
- **Placement:** Lesson 7 (Slide 6 — Scene 6: Turning the Tide)
- **Story Purpose:** Provides the visual payoff following the community's combined persuasive effort, showing the ancient floodgates opening as seawater is safely diverted.
- **Duration:** 15–20 seconds

#### 🎥 AI Visual Prompt (Text-to-Video Engine)
> `High-contrast cinematic shot of massive ancient iron floodgate gears slowly turning in unison. High-pressure ocean water roaring harmlessly down a reinforced spillway tunnel into the sea. Golden sunrise breaking through sea mist over Wittling harbour in the background, dramatic epic lighting, motion blur on churning gears, photorealistic 8k.`

#### 🎙️ Audio / Voiceover Prompt (TTS Script)
> `[Sound: Heavy gear rumble, roaring spillway water, cheering crowd fading in] Narrator (triumphant, inspiring tone): "The Grandfather Valve opens. Through unity and persuasive argument, Wittling-on-Sea stands safe."`
